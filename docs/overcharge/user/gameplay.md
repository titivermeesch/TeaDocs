# Gameplay

## A match

![Players fighting over cells in the middle](/img/overcharge/middle.png)

1. Players join with `/play overcharge`. When enough are in, the countdown starts.
2. Each player joins a team and starts at their team's base. The compass in the last hotbar slot always points to your station.
3. Energy cells (glowing amethyst shards) drop at the energy points in the middle, one every few seconds, until 12 lie on the ground.
4. Walk over a cell to pick it up. Each cell is worth 5 charge, and you carry up to 50. Your charge shows on the XP bar.
5. Crouch on your team's station to move your charge into the battery, 5 per second.
6. The first battery to reach 100 wins.

![A team station](/img/overcharge/station.png)

If time runs out (10 minutes), the fullest battery wins. On a tie the match goes to overtime: the next team to deliver any charge wins.

## Carrying and dying

![Carriers heading home](/img/overcharge/field.png)

- When you die, 75% of the charge you carried drops as cells where you fell. The rest is lost. Falling into the void loses all of it.
- Dropped cells scatter, and nobody can pick them up for 1.5 seconds. After that anyone can, your team or theirs.
- You glow when you carry more than half the cap, so the other teams see you through walls.
- You respawn at your base after 3 seconds, with 3 seconds of protection.
- Teammates can't hurt each other.

## Kits

| Kit | Gear | Plays like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Runner | A Shove Stick (Knockback II) and boots, Speed II | Grabs cells first and runs them home. |
| Brawler | Stone sword, chainmail chestplate, Resistance I | Guards the middle and the station. |
| Zapper | Wooden sword and the Stun Gun | Stops carriers from a distance. |

**Stun Gun.** Right-click to zap the first enemy you aim at, up to 10 blocks away. The target is slowed for 2 seconds and drops up to 10 charge as cells. The gun recharges for 6 seconds. A miss still costs you the shot.

## Leaving

If every player of all but one team leaves, the remaining team wins. If someone leaves while carrying charge, it drops where they stood.
