# What is Overcharge

Overcharge is a team minigame built on [Tea](../tea/intro.md). Energy cells drop in the middle of the map. You grab them, run them back to your base, and crouch on your team's station to charge your battery. The first team to fill its battery wins.

![Substation, the built-in map](/img/overcharge/arena.png)

You can carry up to 50 charge, and dying drops most of it on the ground for anyone to grab. Carriers with a full load glow, so everyone can see who to chase.

Two to four teams play, depending on the map. The kits change how you play: Runner is fast, Brawler is tough, and Zapper has a stun gun that knocks charge loose.
