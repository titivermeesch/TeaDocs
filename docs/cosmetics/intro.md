# What is TeaCosmetics

TeaCosmetics adds cosmetics to every game built on [Tea](../tea/intro.md). Players buy them with coins in the `/cosmetics` menu and wear one of each type:

| Type | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Projectile trail | Particles follow arrows, fireballs and anything else you shoot. |
| Walk trail | Particles at your feet while you move, in the lobby and in matches. |
| Kill effect | Plays where a player you killed died: lightning, a firework, particles or a sound. |
| Win effect | Plays around you when your team (or you) wins a match. |
| Hat | Worn on your head in the lobby. Matches take it off, since games decide what goes on the head. |
| Death message | A line in the match chat announcing your kills, such as "Steve was roasted by Alex". |
| Victory dance | You spin, bounce, blast off like a rocket or get circled by particles when you win. |

![The /cosmetics menu](/img/cosmetics/menu.png)

See [The cosmetics](./user/cosmetics.md) for every effect.

Cosmetics change what players see and nothing else. Nobody can be hurt by a cosmetic firework or lightning bolt, and spectators and invisible players leave no trails.

TeaCosmetics is a separate plugin, so you install it only if you want cosmetics. It works with every Tea game and needs no setup inside the game.
