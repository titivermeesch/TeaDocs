# What is Fireball

Fireball is a team minigame built on [Tea](../tea/intro.md). Two to eight teams fight with throwable fireballs in a timed match. Every kill scores a point for your team. When the clock runs out, the team with the most kills wins.

You charge your throws, jump on your own blasts, swat incoming fireballs back, fire map cannons, grab power-ups and spend the coins you earn from kills in an in-match shop. There are 11 fireball types, from a plain blast to a black hole that pulls enemies in before it bursts.

Fireball is a single plugin that depends on TeaCore. Tea provides the lobby, matchmaking, maps, kits and cross-server routing.
